Events

Alberta’s weaving community hosts three different types of events throughout the year.

Open weaves do not have an instructor present and are free to attend. You can bring your own materials or coordinate with the host beforehand, and they can provide you with materials for a small gathering fee.

Workshops are led by experienced instructors, and materials are included in the price. Most workshops range between $90 (half day) to $425 (multi-day).

Retreats span several days and are centred on either a series of workshops or one larger project. They can include catered meals and accommodations, or access to camping.

Harvesting Hints

A bit of foresight into what you can harvest now and in the months ahead.

Cat Tails

August - September

When the tips begin to brown, before the first frost.

Ditch Willow

November - March

When at least 2/3 of leaves have fallen in the fall and before the buds swell in the spring.

Dogwood

November - March

Thin, flexible branches, thinner than a pencil, once the leaves are off.
