Membership

Basketry is for everyone

Our suggested membership fee is $50. This fee allows us to sustain our community activities and benefits. If this fee is not within your means, please only pay what you can afford. Or, if you can afford a little more, then help us cover the members who can’t. Every dollar makes a difference.

$1 $25 $50 $75

$50 is the suggested amount — any of these joins you.

Thank you for whatever support you can give — and thank you for joining!

What membership gives you

  • Resources and inspiration to support you on your weaving journey
  • Access to our private Slack space — connect with fellow members, ask questions, share ideas, and stay in touch between events
  • Discounts for Guild Sponsored Events, and Members Socials
  • Priority registration for Weaving Connections
  • Spotlight Features — members are invited to apply to be featured in our newsletter and on our social media, so we can all get to know the talented individuals behind the classes, studios, and businesses that keep basketry alive and thriving in Alberta
  • A warm, supportive community of like-minded weavers who cherish the rhythm and connection of working with natural materials

Managing your membership

Our membership operates as an annual subscription. Your credit card will be automatically charged upon renewal next year. You will receive a reminder email one month before your subscription is renewed. If you are charged before you have a chance to cancel, you can always request a refund by emailing albertabasketryguild@gmail.com.

Log in to Stripe to confirm your membership and update your membership preferences.
