Submit an Event

Share your upcoming basket weaving workshops in Alberta, Canada.

Our website is maintained by our community members. You do not need to be a guild member to advertise your event.

Please email your event info to submissions@albertabasketryguild.com.

Please note that updating of events is dependent on the availability of volunteers to add your events, and sometimes there can be a bit of a lag.

Event info to include

Copy this into your email and fill in what you can.

Date

The day, or the first and last day if it runs over more than one.

Event name

What you are calling it.

Teacher or business name

Name of the instructor or business.

City or town

Where the event is taking place.

Type of event

Workshop, open weave, art show, craft sale, festival, retreat or other.

Venue name

The name of the place — we link to your registration page for directions, so there is no need to send us a street address.

How people sign up

Your online registration link.

Or a registration contact name and email address.

Or a link to the waitlist, if it has sold out.

Photo (optional)

A horizontal 16 × 9 photo. Please only send photos you have the right to share.
