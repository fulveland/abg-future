Submit an Opportunity

Looking for a weaver, or need a few extra hands?

Are you looking for a basket weaver? Or do you have a basketry related project that could use a few extra hands?

Send us an email with the details to submissions@albertabasketryguild.com and we will include you on our opportunities page.

Our website is maintained by our community members, and adding your listing depends on a volunteer being free to do it — so there can sometimes be a bit of a lag.

What to include

Copy this into your email and fill in what you can.

What you are asking for

A short title — “Willow harvest volunteers”, “Weaver wanted for a commission”.

Kind

A word or two: volunteer, teaching, harvest, call for weavers, or paid work.

When

A date, a range of dates, or tell us it is ongoing.

City or town

Where it is, or let us know if it can be anywhere in Alberta.

Venue name (if there is one)

The name of the place is plenty — no need for a street address.

Who is asking

Your name, or the name of your organisation.

A sentence or two

What it involves, and anything a weaver would want to know before getting in touch — tools provided, experience needed, whether it is paid.

How to reach you

An email address, or a link to your own page about it.

Photo (optional)

A horizontal 16 × 9 photo. Please only send photos you have the right to share.

Something else in mind?

Running a workshop, retreat, show or sale instead? Those go on our events list — see submit an event.

If you would like to book someone to teach, see our current instructor list.
